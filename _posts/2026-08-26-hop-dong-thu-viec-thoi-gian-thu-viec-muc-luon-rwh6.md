# Tra cứu cơ sở dữ liệu pháp luật lao động hiện hành về: Quy định hợp đồng thử việc, thời gian thử việ

*Note: The specific maximum probation periods for each job qualification (such as 180 days for executive/managerial positions, 60 days for highly skilled positions, etc.) are not explicitly detailed in the provided database. However, the database strictly regulates compliance, probation limits, and penalties for violations.*

### 1. Key Legal Requirements & Compliance Rules

*   **Probation Salary Limit**: The salary paid to an employee during the probation period must be agreed upon by both parties but must be **at least 85%** of the official salary of that job [^4]. The official salary is determined based on the job or title according to the salary scale or table established by the employer [^14]. For specific labor supply contracts, the probation salary must also be clearly specified [^7].
*   **Short-term Contracts**: Employers are strictly prohibited from requiring a probation period for employees who are hired under a labor contract with a term of **less than 1 month** [^4].
*   **Probation Frequency**: An employer is only allowed to require **one probation period** for a single job [^4]. Requiring multiple probations for the same job is a violation [^4].
*   **Notification of Results**: Upon the completion of the probation period, the employer is legally required to notify the employee of the probation results [^4].
*   **Transition to Official Contract**: If the employee's probation meets the requirements, the employer must immediately enter into an official labor contract with the employee (in cases where a separate probation contract was signed) [^4].

### 3. Notes & Practical Tips

*   **Remedial Measures**: In addition to administrative fines, employers who violate probation regulations (such as underpaying probationers, extending probation illegally, or testing short-term workers) are subject to compulsory remedial measures. They must pay the employee the remaining amount to match the full 100% salary of that job for the violated period [^4].
*   **HR Best Practices**: To mitigate legal risks, HR departments should establish a standardized probation evaluation workflow. This includes issuing a written "Notification of Probation Results" before the probation period ends and ensuring that the official labor contract is executed immediately upon successful completion.
