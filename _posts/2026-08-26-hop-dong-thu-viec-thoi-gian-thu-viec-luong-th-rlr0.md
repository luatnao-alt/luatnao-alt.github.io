## 📝 1. Overview & Legal Basis
- The provided legal database does not contain the general provisions of the Vietnamese Labor Code regarding maximum probation periods (such as 180, 60, 30, or 6 days) or the minimum probationary salary rate (85% of the official salary). However, specific rules regarding probation termination and internal labor compliance are regulated.
- According to Circular 5/2023/TT-BNV Article 4 Clause 4 ([Termination of labor contract during probation due to failure to meet requirements]), the employer (Party A) reserves the right to unilaterally terminate the labor contract before the expiration of the probation period if the employee (Party B) fails to meet the job requirements [^6].
- Additionally, under Consolidated Document 4759/VBHN-BLĐTBXH Article 27 ([Contents of internal labor regulations]), any employee undergoing probation must strictly comply with the established internal labor regulations, including working hours, occupational safety, and asset protection [^7].

## 💡 3. Notes & Practical Tips (Exceptions & Next Steps)
- **Exceptions (trừ trường hợp)**: Under Circular 5/2023/TT-BNV Article 4 Clause 4, the employer can terminate the contract without waiting for the probation period to end, provided that the employee's performance does not meet the requirements [^6].

Failure to provide safety gear during probation poses severe compliance risks.
